A FEW GOOD POINTS

Corners make good landmarks.

Choose sharp, stationary details spread across the overlap. Aim for 8–12 pairs. Avoid points all on one line.

Click a numbered marker to adjust its position, then click the corrected location. Use “Delete pair” to remove a match from both photos.

Coordinates use the loaded image’s original dimensions, with (0, 0) at the top-left pixel center. Each pair is [x, y]. Resize photos before selecting points if your stitching code uses smaller images.
